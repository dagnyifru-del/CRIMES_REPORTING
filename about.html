About Us

Empowering communities through technology and transparency.

Ethiopian Police University (EPU), originally established as Aba Dina Police College in 1946, has undergone significant transformation over the decades, achieving full university status in December 2020.

Our Vision

To build a safer and more transparent society by enabling citizens and law enforcement to collaborate effectively through digital innovation.

Our Mission

To provide a reliable platform for reporting crimes, managing investigations, and strengthening trust between the public and police institutions.
